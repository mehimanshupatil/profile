## What it is

All 37 stations and the track geometry are baked from OpenStreetMap and draped over a real terrain heightfield, with the Arabian Sea, Mahim bay and the Vasai creek crossing where they belong.

## The hard part

Every slow, fast and AC local runs the actual Western Railway Public Time Table: 1,358 real services (837 slow, 374 fast, 147 AC) baked from the official PDFs. That covers real departure times, turnback termini and skip-stop patterns. Fast locals overtake slows because the schedules say so; nothing is scripted.

## How

A pure, deterministic simulation core, `(network, services, simTime) → TrainState[]`, drives everything. A sim clock (pause, 1×, 10×, 60×, or sync to live IST) runs both the timetable and a day/night cycle.
