## What it is

A toolbox for merging, splitting, compressing and watermarking PDFs, converting, resizing and cropping images, background removal, and trimming and converting video and audio. Every tool runs client-side, so files never leave the device.

## The hard part

Doing server-grade work in a tab: FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly for video and audio, pdf-lib and PDF.js for documents, and an ONNX model for on-device background removal, all inside an installable PWA built from static Astro pages with React islands.
